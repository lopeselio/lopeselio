## The studio years

In 2021 I co-founded [IntoTheVerse](https://github.com/IntoTheVerse), a game studio. We were picked for Celo Camp that year, and over the next four years the team grew to 16 people and shipped games across six chains.

- **Intrant Inferis**: a story-driven dungeon RPG for mobile on Solana, which we took to Solana Breakpoint in Amsterdam for live playtests.
- **Cryptara Conquest**: a fully on-chain survival shooter. It won the Solana Speedrun Game Jam, launched on Eclipse mainnet in January 2025, and reached 13K+ players and 2M+ transactions in its first six weeks. It later came to Android through the Solana dApp Store.
- **[Sushi Nads](https://sushinads.fun)**: a cooking game on Monad with gasless play and on-chain scoring.

Along the way the studio took 1st place in the games track at Solana Summer Camp, Aptos Hack Singapore, the Solana Speedrun Game Jam and the Solana Bonkathon.

## Fixing the tools I depended on

Shipping a Unity game to a phone meant running into gaps in the tools, so I started closing them in the open. In [Solana.Unity-SDK](https://github.com/magicblock-labs/Solana.Unity-SDK) that meant wallet signing for Solana Seeker, readable `.skr` names in place of wallet addresses, and a fix for social login. Each one came from a problem in a real game.

I also publish smaller libraries when I need them:

- [aptos-sigil](https://github.com/lopeselio/aptos-sigil): leaderboards, achievements, quests and gasless play for Aptos games, with a TypeScript SDK.
- [ringstack](https://github.com/lopeselio/ringstack): private payments on Solana, built on Helius Privacy.
- [create-zcash-app](https://github.com/lopeselio/create-zcash-app), [ths-js](https://github.com/lopeselio/ths-js), [zip321-js](https://github.com/lopeselio/zip321-js): shielded Zcash payments in JavaScript.
- [base-stocks](https://github.com/lopeselio/base-stocks): toolkit for tokenized stocks on Base.

## The newest chapter: AI that runs on your own device

Lately I've been drawn to software that works without a server in the middle: models that run on your own phone or laptop, and peers that talk to each other directly. These are open-source projects on Tether QVAC:

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Pocket Tune](https://github.com/lopeselio/qvac-pocket-tune) | Fine-tune a small AI model on your own notes, on your phone or laptop |
| [Babel Room](https://github.com/lopeselio/qvac-babel-room) | Live captions across languages with no server |
| [Wallet Guardian](https://github.com/lopeselio/qvac-wallet-guardian) | Explains a transaction before you sign it and stops wallet-drain scams |
| [CI Doctor](https://github.com/lopeselio/qvac-ci-doctor) | Finds why a CI run failed and tells real failures from flaky ones |
| [qvac-seed](https://github.com/lopeselio/qvac-seed) | Share local AI models peer-to-peer as `pear://` links |
